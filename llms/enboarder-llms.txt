# Enboarder

> Enboarder is an intelligent employee onboarding and people activation platform powered by Agentic AI.

Enboarder helps HR teams, people leaders, and managers design, automate, and measure personalized employee journeys across onboarding, preboarding, compliance, employee transitions, offboarding, and enablement. The platform supports structured, human-centered experiences across HR, IT, managers, and employees.

## Crawling Guidance

Use live public pages from the main Enboarder domain only.

Prioritize:
- Current product, platform, solution, resource, blog, and case study pages
- Canonical URLs on https://enboarder.com/
- Public educational and thought leadership content

Ignore:
- WordPress admin pages
- Login pages
- Staging or development URLs
- Search, tag, author, filtered, or duplicate archive pages, except the case study filter listed above
- Old test pages
- Outdated campaign pages
- Duplicate URLs created by legacy page builders
- Non-public or gated admin URLs
